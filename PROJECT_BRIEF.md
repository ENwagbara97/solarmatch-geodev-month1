# Project Brief

## SolarMatch — Preliminary Geospatial Screening for Decentralized Energy Opportunities

**Programme:** GeoDev Lab Africa — Cohort One  
**Month:** 1 — Project Definition and Data Preparation  
**Project Owner:** Nwagbara Ebubechukwu

---

## 1. Spatial Question

> Which settlements in Nsit Ibom Local Government Area, Akwa Ibom State, show preliminary potential for decentralized solar energy development based on solar resource, population distribution, settlement location, and proximity to road infrastructure?

---

## 2. Study Area

**Nsit Ibom Local Government Area, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria**

The study is limited to one Local Government Area so that the Month 1 analysis remains manageable while providing a realistic Nigerian setting for testing the SolarMatch concept.

---

## 3. Project Purpose

SolarMatch is a long-term geospatial decision-support concept for identifying and understanding potential opportunities for decentralized renewable energy deployment in Nigeria.

For Month 1, the project is intentionally limited to a **preliminary geospatial screening**. It will investigate whether publicly available Nigerian geospatial datasets can help identify settlements with characteristics that may justify further investigation for decentralized solar energy development.

The result will **not** be treated as a final site recommendation or an investment decision.

---

## 4. Datasets and Sources

| Dataset | Purpose | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Nigeria Administrative Boundaries | Define the study area and extract Nsit Ibom LGA | [ENERGYDATA.INFO — Nigeria Administrative Boundaries](https://energydata.info/en/dataset/nigeria-administrative-boundaries-2017) |
| Solar Resource Data | Represent the available solar resource across the study area | [Global Solar Atlas — Data Sources](https://dev.globalsolaratlas.info/support/data-sources) |
| Population Raster | Represent population distribution as an indicative proxy for potential energy demand | [WorldPop — Nigeria Population Data](https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/summary?id=74736) |
| Settlement Data | Identify populated places and settlement locations | [OpenStreetMap — Export](https://www.openstreetmap.org/export/) |
| Road Network | Represent road infrastructure and preliminary accessibility | [OpenStreetMap — Downloading Data](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Downloading_data) |

**Note:** Dataset versions, dates, spatial resolutions, coordinate reference systems, and limitations will be recorded after the data are inspected and downloaded.

---

## 5. Planned Approach

1. Confirm the spatial question and study area.
2. Locate and obtain the listed datasets.
3. Inspect vector and raster data and their attributes/metadata.
4. Verify coordinate reference systems and spatial reference information.
5. Extract or clip the datasets to Nsit Ibom LGA where appropriate.
6. Examine relationships among settlements, population, solar resource, and road infrastructure.
7. Perform a first spatial screening operation using the available data.
8. Document assumptions, limitations, and the analytical workflow.
9. Produce a preliminary map/result showing areas that may warrant further investigation.

The exact analysis will be determined after inspecting the real datasets rather than assuming that every dataset can be used in the same way.

---

## 6. Month 1 Scope

### Included

- Study area boundary
- Settlement locations
- Population distribution
- Solar resource
- Road infrastructure
- Basic spatial relationships
- CRS verification
- Preliminary spatial screening

### Not included

- Detailed solar PV system design
- Electrical engineering design
- Detailed electricity demand/load modelling
- Distribution network design
- Land ownership or land availability assessment
- Environmental and social impact assessment
- Community consent
- Financial feasibility
- Regulatory approval
- Final investment or construction decisions

---

## 7. Expected Outcome

The Month 1 project should demonstrate that the selected datasets can be obtained, prepared, referenced correctly, and used for an initial spatial analysis relevant to decentralized energy planning.

The output will identify **candidate areas for further investigation**, not locations where a project should automatically be built.

---

## 8. Key Limitations

The screening may be affected by differences in dataset dates and spatial resolution, incomplete OpenStreetMap features, uncertainty in population estimates, and the absence of detailed site-level engineering, electricity demand, land, environmental, social, financial, and regulatory information.

A favourable preliminary result therefore does not establish technical, financial, social, environmental, or legal suitability.
